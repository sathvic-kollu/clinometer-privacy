Privacy Policy — Clinometer

Applies to the Android app app.clinometer.anglemeter.bubblelevel.
Last updated: 7 November 2026.

The short version

Clinometer does not have accounts, does not have a server, and does not upload your measurements, your photos, or your location anywhere. Everything you measure stays on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is what the advertising SDKs collect, and you control that through the consent choice the app shows you.

What stays entirely on your device

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Camera — optional. Only used to show a live backdrop behind the gauge. Every angle, height and distance is computed from the tilt sensor, so the app works fully with the camera switched off. Requested only when you turn the camera view on.
  • Approximate location — optional and off by default. Requested only at the moment you enable coordinate stamping in Settings. Only the last known position is read; the app never tracks you and never runs location in the background.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — needed to save a measurement photo. On Android 10 and newer no storage permission is used at all.
  • Internet, network state, advertising ID — used by the advertising SDKs described below.
  • Vibrate — the small buzz when a reading locks.

No permission is required to use the app. Declining any of them leaves the measuring features working.

Advertising

Clinometer is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. The Samsung Galaxy Store build 1.0.4 (version code 5) includes Google's advertising SDK and does not include the AppLovin or Meta Audience Network mediation SDKs. The Google Play build of this version also includes the Meta Audience Network mediation SDK, but not AppLovin.

Older installed versions may have a different mediation configuration. The AppLovin and Meta privacy links below are retained for versions that include those SDKs. The consent message lists the ad technology partners that may receive data through Google ad delivery; these can include partners without a separate SDK in the app.

Advertising partners collect and process data used to select and measure ads. That typically includes your device's advertising identifier, device and network information, and coarse location derived from your IP address. The optional coordinate stamp described above is a separate local feature; the app does not send those coordinates in ad requests.

Where the law requires it, the app shows you a consent form the first time it runs, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice is recorded and honoured. You can change it at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID from Android's own settings, which limits ad personalisation across every app on the device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email collection or user profiles.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No separate tracking or data-broker SDK beyond the advertising SDKs described above. The Google Play build includes the Install Referrer library described below.
  • No push notifications and no notification permission.
  • We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners process the information described in the Advertising section.

Install attribution

The Google Play version reads the Play Install Referrer once, so we can tell which listing or campaign an install came from. It is stored on the device and is not transmitted. The Samsung Galaxy Store version does not include this at all.

Children

Clinometer is a general-purpose measuring tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it — though note that the app provides no way to submit personal information in the first place.

Your rights

Because the app holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it; to delete saved measurement photos, delete them from within the app or from your gallery. For rights relating to advertising data, contact the relevant advertising partner using the links above, or use Android's advertising ID controls.
