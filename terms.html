Accuracy and intended use

Clinometer measures using the accelerometer built into your phone. Its accuracy is limited by that hardware. After calibration you can typically expect readings within a few tenths of a degree on a steady surface — good enough for building, carpentry, DIY, landscaping, panel alignment and similar work.

It is not a certified or calibrated instrument. Do not rely on it alone where an incorrect measurement could cause injury, structural failure, or significant financial loss. For work of that kind, verify with a purpose-built instrument.

Readings can also be wrong if the phone is moving, if it has not been calibrated, if it is in a case that prevents it sitting flat, or if the surface you are measuring against is not what you believe it to be. The app warns you when it detects motion, but it cannot detect every source of error.
